# The 52-Week Year-Round Plan
1 comprehensive homework set of 10 questions per week over a 1-year timeline.
Topic Split: 25 sets focused on Number (Arithmetic, Fractions, Primes, Ratio), 10 sets on Top-School Selective Reasoning, and 17 sets distributed across Algebra, Space/Measure, Data, and Sequences.

## 📅 The 52-Week Year-Round Study Plan & Curriculum
The complete plan covers the comprehensive 11+ syllabus, structured into four strategic terms of 13 weeks each:
### Term 1 (Weeks 1–13): Arithmetic & Number Sense Foundations
Focus: Place values, column addition/subtraction, long multiplication, bus stop division, order of operations (BODMAS/BIDMAS), primes, factors, HCF/LCM, square/cube roots, fractions, and decimal conversions.
### Term 2 (Weeks 14–26): Proportional Reasoning & Basics of Algebra
Focus: Percentage conversions, percentage of amounts, percentage increase/decrease, ratio sharing, scale drawings/maps, direct/inverse proportion, simplifying variables, single & two-step equations, and linear nth-term sequences.
### Term 3 (Weeks 27–39): Shape, Space, Measures & Data Handling
Focus: Intersecting line angles, interior polygon angles, perimeter and area of compound shapes, volume and surface area of cuboids, 3D shape vertices, coordinates, metric/imperial conversions, clock elapsed time, speed/distance/time, and interpreting statistical charts.
## Term 4 (Weeks 40–52): Advanced Problem Solving, Non-Routine Reasoning & Exam Mastery
Focus: Multi-step worded problems, Venn & Carroll diagrams, probability outcomes, digit cryptarithms, shared work rates, age timeline puzzles, pigeonhole principle, exam-timing strategies, math-verbal crossover codes, and mock exam review.
