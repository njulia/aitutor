#### Q1. What is the value of the digit 7 in the number 2,374,905?
**Options:**
   A) Seventy
   B) Seven Hundred
   C) Seventy Thousand
   D) Seven Thousand
   E) Seven Million

**Correct Answer:** Option **C** (Seventy Thousand)

**Worked Step-by-Step Explanation:**
In the number 2,374,905, looking at place value from right to left: 5 is units, 0 is tens, 9 is hundreds, 4 is thousands, 7 is ten-thousands (70,000), 3 is hundred-thousands, and 2 is millions. Therefore, the 7 represents seventy thousand.

**Coach Pip's Elite School Tip:** *Write out the column headings (M, HTh, TTh, Th, H, T, U) above the digits if you're ever unsure about placing!*

*(This week includes 10 selective-school style practice questions)*

#### Q1. Solve: 456 ÷ 8 = ?
**Options:**
   A) 56
   B) 57
   C) 58
   D) 67
   E) 57.5

**Correct Answer:** Option **B** (57)

**Worked Step-by-Step Explanation:**
Using short division (the 'bus stop' method):
- 8 into 4 doesn't go (carry 4 to make 45).
- 8 into 45 goes 5 times, remainder 5 (write 5 above, carry 5 to make 56).
- 8 into 56 goes exactly 7 times.
- The quotient is 57.

**Coach Pip's Elite School Tip:** *If you divide by 8, you can also half the number three times in a row! Half of 456 is 228, half of 228 is 114, half of 114 is 57. This is incredibly reliable!*

#### Q2. What is the remainder when 389 is divided by 6?

## Week 5: Order of Operations (BODMAS/BIDMAS)
* **Syllabus Area:** Number: Arithmetic & Mental Maths
* **Learning Objectives:**
  - Understand priority of brackets, indices, division/multiplication, and addition/subtraction.
  - Evaluate complex multi-step expressions.
  - Insert missing brackets to make equations true.

### 📝 Homework Set 5

*(This week includes 10 selective-school style practice questions)*

#### Q1. Solve: (12 - 4) × 5 + 3 = ?
**Options:**
   A) 43
   B) 61
   C) 17
   D) 67
   E) 53

**Correct Answer:** Option **A** (43)

**Worked Step-by-Step Explanation:**
According to BODMAS/BIDMAS, perform operations inside brackets first:
1) Brackets: 12 - 4 = 8
2) Multiplication next: 8 × 5 = 40
3) Addition last: 40 + 3 = 43.

**Coach Pip's Elite School Tip:** *Always underline or write down the portion of the formula you are solving on each line to keep track!*

---
#### Q2. Solve: 24 - 12 ÷ 3 × 2 + 5 = ?
**Options:**
   A) 13
   B) 19
   C) 21
   D) 2
   E) 3

**Correct Answer:** Option **C** (21)

**Worked Step-by-Step Explanation:**
According to BODMAS, division and multiplication have equal priority and are performed from left to right, followed by addition and subtraction:
1) Division: 12 ÷ 3 = 4. The expression becomes: 24 - 4 × 2 + 5
2) Multiplication: 4 × 2 = 8. Expression becomes: 24 - 8 + 5
3) Addition/Subtraction from left to right: 24 - 8 = 16, then 16 + 5 = 21.

**Coach Pip's Elite School Tip:** *A classic 11+ trap is doing 24 - 12 first, or doing 3 × 2 first. Always follow BODMAS rigidly!*

---
#### Q3. Where should brackets be placed in the following expression to make it correct? 4 + 6 × 3 - 2 = 28

   D) (4 + 6 × 3) - 2

**Correct Answer:** Option **A** ((4 + 6) × 3 - 2)

**Worked Step-by-Step Explanation:**
Let's test option A: (4 + 6) × 3 - 2.
- Solve inside brackets first: 4 + 6 = 10
