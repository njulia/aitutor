Year 5 Homework Help & Practice

Year 5 is an important preparation year for Year 6 and, for some families, the 11+. Homework Magic combines everyday homework support with focused practice and mistake review.

Start Year 5 practice

What can children practise in Year 5?

Homework Magic supports Maths, English, reading, grammar, spelling, Science and 11+ preparation. Use short practice sessions to reinforce school learning and help children become more confident.

  • Fractions, decimals, percentages and problem solving
  • Reading comprehension and vocabulary
  • Grammar, punctuation and spelling
  • 11+ Maths, English, Verbal and Non-Verbal Reasoning

Year 5 homework support

Use Homework Magic alongside your child's school homework. When a question is difficult, focus on understanding the method, then try another question. Reviewing mistakes can help children see which topics need more practice.

For families preparing for selective-school entrance, the separate 11+ section provides targeted practice, mistake review, topic mastery and mock exams.

Why use short practice sessions?

Short, focused activities can make it easier to keep practice manageable around school, reading, clubs and family time. Children can practise a skill, check their work and return to a tricky topic later.

Frequently asked questions about Year 5

What should Year 5 children practise?

Year 5 practice can include Maths, fractions and decimals, problem solving, reading comprehension, grammar, spelling and writing.

When should a child start 11+ preparation?

There is no single correct starting point. Some families begin during Year 4 or Year 5, depending on the schools they are considering and the child's needs.

Can Homework Magic help with Year 5 11+ practice?

Yes. Homework Magic has a dedicated 11+ area with practice, mistakes, topic mastery and mock exams.
