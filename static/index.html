How Homework Magic works

Set it up once, then let your child practise while you check their progress and decide what to do next.

1

Create a parent account

Sign up and manage your child's learning from your parent account.

2

Add your child

Choose a nickname and Year group. Your child can then use their learner login.

3

Choose what to practise

Use Make Homework for everyday learning or 11+ Practice for exam preparation.

4

Review and choose next

Look at answers, explanations and progress. Then repeat a tricky topic or move on.

Good to know

Homework Magic questions

Which school years does Homework Magic support?

Homework Magic is designed for UK primary children in Years 1–6, broadly ages 5–11. 11+ preparation is available for older primary learners.

What subjects can children practise?

Children can practise Maths, English, Science and other school subjects. 11+ practice includes Maths, English, verbal reasoning and non-verbal reasoning.

Is Homework Magic free?

You can start with the available free learning activities. Paid plans provide additional features and more intensive 11+ preparation.

Do parents need to plan every lesson?

No. Start with a short activity, review the result and choose the next topic from there. Parents should manage the account and supervise children's use.

Does it follow the UK school curriculum?

Primary homework activities are designed around National Curriculum subjects and year groups in England. AI can make mistakes, so parents or teachers should check important information.

Where can I find homework for kids?

Choose your child's Year group and a subject to start a short primary school homework activity. A parent or guardian should manage the account and help choose what is right for their child.
