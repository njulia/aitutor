Built from a real family need with love 🏡

Why I started Homework Magic

I’m a parent whose daughter is starting Year 3 in September 2026. Her school does not set regular homework, but I wanted to give her a simple 10–15 minutes of useful practice each day.

The materials I found were often one-size-fits-all, and I still needed to choose the right work and check every answer myself. So I began building an AI tutor that could prepare practice for her level and give helpful feedback straight away, at any time.

My daughter liked the idea and helped shape the design and choose the name Homework Magic. Her input is a daily reminder that learning should feel friendly, manageable and made for the child using it.

Good to know

Questions families ask

Which ages and school years can use Homework Magic?

It is designed for UK primary learners in Years 1–6, broadly ages 5–11, with extra 11+ preparation for older primary learners.

Does it follow the school curriculum?

Homework activities are designed around National Curriculum subjects and year groups in England. A parent, guardian or teacher should still check anything important because AI can make mistakes.

What can I use before choosing a paid plan?

Families can try available curriculum questions. Parent plans add features such as Tutor mode, deeper explanations, progress and targeted practice.

How should a child use the website safely?

A parent or guardian should manage the account. Use a learner nickname and never enter a child’s school, address, phone number, exact birthday or password into a homework box.

Does this replace a teacher?

No. Homework Magic is a practice helper. Teachers and parents know the child and should make important learning or wellbeing decisions.
