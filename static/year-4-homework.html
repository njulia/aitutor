Year 4 Homework Help & Practice

Year 4 is a key KS2 year for building fluency and confidence. Homework Magic helps children practise Maths and English in manageable sessions and review areas they find difficult.

Start Year 4 practice

What can children practise in Year 4?

Homework Magic supports Maths, English, reading, grammar, spelling and Science. Use short practice sessions to reinforce school learning and help children become more confident.

  • Multiplication, division and fractions
  • Decimals and problem solving
  • Reading comprehension and vocabulary
  • Grammar, punctuation and spelling

Year 4 homework support

Use Homework Magic alongside your child's school homework. When a question is difficult, focus on understanding the method, then try another question. Reviewing mistakes can help children see which topics need more practice.

For families preparing for selective-school entrance, the separate 11+ section provides targeted practice, mistake review, topic mastery and mock exams.

Why use short practice sessions?

Short, focused activities can make it easier to keep practice manageable around school, reading, clubs and family time. Children can practise a skill, check their work and return to a tricky topic later.

Frequently asked questions about Year 4

What should a Year 4 child practise?

Year 4 practice can include multiplication and division, fractions and decimals, problem solving, reading, grammar, punctuation and spelling.

How can Year 4 children improve Maths?

Regular short practice, reviewing mistakes and practising a skill in different question styles can help build confidence.

Is Year 4 a good time to explore 11+ skills?

Families considering selective-school entrance may choose to explore 11+ skills during KS2. Homework Magic provides a separate 11+ practice area.
