Check My Homework — Instant AI Marking

Upload a photo of your child's handwritten homework, submit a file, or type in their answers directly. Homework Magic will mark it instantly and provide detailed, encouraging feedback that explains what they did well, where mistakes happened, and how to improve.

Try the Homework Checker

How It Works

  1. Generate or bring your own homework — Use Homework Magic to create personalised questions, or use work from school.
  2. Have your child complete the work — On paper, digitally, or any format they prefer.
  3. Submit for marking — Upload a photo, submit a file, or type answers directly into the text box.
  4. Get instant feedback — Receive detailed, encouraging review with scores, explanations, and next steps.

Three Ways to Submit

📝

Text Input

Type or paste homework questions and answers directly into the form.

📁

File Upload

Upload documents in TXT, MD, CSV, or PDF format for automatic review.

📸

Photo Upload

Take a photo of handwritten work — the AI reads and marks it automatically.

What the Feedback Includes

  • Encouragement: Positive feedback on what your child did well.
  • Score: A clear score out of 10 to track progress.
  • Explanations: Clear explanations of any mistakes, in language children understand.
  • Correct answers: The right approach or answer shown clearly.
  • Next steps: Suggestions for what to practice next.

Supported File Formats

Upload homework in any of these formats:

JPG PNG HEIC GIF PDF TXT MD CSV

Handwritten work works best when the photo is well-lit and the writing is clear. Maximum file size: 16 MB.

Subjects Covered

The homework checker supports all UK primary school subjects:

  • Mathematics: Arithmetic, fractions, geometry, problem-solving
  • English: Comprehension, grammar, punctuation, vocabulary, creative writing
  • Science: Biology, chemistry, physics topics
  • Humanities: History, Geography, RE
  • Languages: Spanish, French, Latin, Chinese
  • 11+ Preparation: Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, Maths, English

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it mark handwritten work?

Yes! Just take a clear photo of your child's homework and upload it — the AI will read and review it using advanced OCR technology.

What file formats are supported?

You can upload images (JPG, PNG, HEIC, GIF), PDF files, or text files (TXT, MD, CSV). The AI will extract and review the content automatically.

What subjects can it mark?

All UK primary school subjects including Maths, English, Science, History, Geography, and 11+ preparation subjects.

Does it replace a teacher?

No — Homework Magic is a supplementary tool to support home learning, not a replacement for school teaching.

Is the feedback encouraging?

Yes! All feedback is designed to be positive and constructive, focusing on what the child did well while clearly explaining areas for improvement.
