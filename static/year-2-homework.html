Year 2 Homework Help & Practice

Year 2 children build confidence across Maths and English while preparing for the end of KS1. Homework Magic provides friendly practice that can fit around school homework and reading.

Start Year 2 practice

What can children practise in Year 2?

Homework Magic supports Maths, English, phonics, reading, spelling and Science. Use short practice sessions to reinforce school learning and help children become more confident.

  • Number, calculation and problem solving
  • Reading, spelling and grammar
  • Writing clear sentences and short pieces
  • Science and topic-based learning

Year 2 homework support

Use Homework Magic alongside your child's school homework. When a question is difficult, focus on understanding the method, then try another question. Reviewing mistakes can help children see which topics need more practice.

For families preparing for selective-school entrance, the separate 11+ section provides targeted practice, mistake review, topic mastery and mock exams.

Why use short practice sessions?

Short, focused activities can make it easier to keep practice manageable around school, reading, clubs and family time. Children can practise a skill, check their work and return to a tricky topic later.

Frequently asked questions about Year 2

What should a Year 2 child practise?

Useful Year 2 practice includes Maths, reading, spelling, grammar, writing and problem solving, alongside the child's school work.

Is Year 2 SATs practice included?

Homework Magic can provide Year 2 learning practice. Use the activities to build skills rather than relying only on test-style questions.

Can Homework Magic explain difficult homework?

Yes. Children can use Homework Magic for friendly explanations and guided practice.
