Year 6 Homework Help & Practice

Year 6 brings a strong focus on consolidation, confidence and independent learning. Homework Magic helps children practise key Maths and English skills, review mistakes and prepare for the next step.

Start Year 6 practice

What can children practise in Year 6?

Homework Magic supports Maths, English, reading, grammar, spelling, SATs-related practice and 11+. Use short practice sessions to reinforce school learning and help children become more confident.

  • Arithmetic and mathematical problem solving
  • Fractions, decimals and percentages
  • Reading comprehension and grammar
  • Revision, mistakes and exam-style practice

Year 6 homework support

Use Homework Magic alongside your child's school homework. When a question is difficult, focus on understanding the method, then try another question. Reviewing mistakes can help children see which topics need more practice.

For families preparing for selective-school entrance, the separate 11+ section provides targeted practice, mistake review, topic mastery and mock exams.

Why use short practice sessions?

Short, focused activities can make it easier to keep practice manageable around school, reading, clubs and family time. Children can practise a skill, check their work and return to a tricky topic later.

Frequently asked questions about Year 6

What should Year 6 children practise?

Year 6 practice can focus on Maths, arithmetic, problem solving, fractions, decimals, percentages, reading, grammar, spelling and writing.

Does Homework Magic support SATs practice?

Homework Magic provides primary Maths and English practice that can help children build the skills they use at the end of KS2.

Can Year 6 children use the 11+ section?

Yes. Families who are preparing for 11+ tests can use the dedicated 11+ practice, mistakes, topic mastery and mock-exam areas.
