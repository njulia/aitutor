A useful reading conversation

Help with the thinking, without giving everything away.

📚

1. Choose a focus

Select English for mixed practice or ask for reading, vocabulary, spelling or grammar.

🔎

2. Find the clues

Encourage the learner to point to words in the passage and explain their own idea first.

🌱

3. Try the next step

Use the review summary to celebrate what worked and choose one small area to practise again.
