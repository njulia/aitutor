Geometry & Algebra Fundamentals

Master essential geometric and algebraic concepts for the 11+ exam.

Practice Maths Questions

2D Shapes

Know properties of triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, and polygons. Understand angles, sides, and symmetry.

3D Shapes

Recognise cubes, cuboids, spheres, cylinders, cones, and pyramids. Understand faces, edges, and vertices.

Angles

Types: acute, right, obtuse, reflex. Angle facts: angles in a triangle sum to 180, angles on a straight line sum to 180.

Area & Perimeter

Perimeter: sum of all sides. Area: squares and rectangles (length x width), triangles (1/2 x base x height).

Algebra

Expressions: combining like terms. Equations: solving for unknown values. Sequences: finding patterns.
