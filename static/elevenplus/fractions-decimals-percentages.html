Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

Master these fundamental 11+ maths topics with conversions and problem-solving techniques.

Practice Maths Questions

Fractions

A fraction represents a part of a whole. Key skills: equivalent fractions, ordering, addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Example: 1/2 = 2/4 = 5/10 (equivalent fractions)

Decimals

Decimals are another way to show parts of a whole. Understanding place value is essential.

Example: 0.5 = 1/2, 0.75 = 3/4

Percentages

Percentages show amounts out of 100. Find percentages of amounts by converting to fractions or decimals.

Example: 25% of 80 = 1/4 x 80 = 20

Conversions

Practice converting between fractions, decimals, and percentages. This is tested frequently in 11+ exams.
