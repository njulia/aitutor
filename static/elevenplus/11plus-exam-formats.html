Understanding 11+ Exam Formats

Know what to expect: different exam boards use different formats.

Start Practising

GL Assessment

Most common format in UK:

English: 50 minutes

Maths: 50 minutes

Reasoning: 50 minutes

Questions: Multiple choice and short answer

CEM (Durham University)

Computer-based adaptive testing:

Test difficulty adapts based on your answers

Shorter papers but covering more breadth

Focus on reasoning and thinking skills

ISEB

Independent Schools Entry Exam:

Often includes essay writing

Longer papers with comprehensive coverage

Used by many independent grammar schools

Key Differences

Check your target school's exam board early. Practice with papers from that specific board for best preparation.

Exam formats and providers can change over time. Always confirm directly with your target school or local authority for the most up-to-date information.
