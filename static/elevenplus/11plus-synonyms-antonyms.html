11+ Synonyms and Antonyms: Vocabulary Practice Guide

Important: 11+ arrangements vary by school and region. Always check the current admissions and test information published by the school, consortium or local authority you are applying to.

Synonyms and antonyms are common vocabulary skills in 11+ preparation. The goal is not just to memorise long word lists: children need to understand how a word behaves in context and recognise the closest meaning.

Synonym strategy

First decide what the word means. Then look for the option with the closest meaning, not simply a word from the same topic. For example, rapid and swift are close in meaning, while race is related but not a synonym.

Antonym strategy

Ask yourself: what is the direct opposite? Watch for near-opposites and words that are related but not true opposites.

Learn words in groups

Group vocabulary by meaning, emotion, movement, size, character and description. Learn a word with a definition, synonym, antonym and example sentence. Then use it in your own sentence.

Daily practice

Ten focused words a day can be more useful than trying to memorise a huge list at once. Revisit older words using spaced repetition so they move into long-term memory.
