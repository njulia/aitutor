Verbal Reasoning Tips & Tricks

Master different verbal reasoning question types with proven strategies.

Practice Verbal Reasoning

Analogies

Create a sentence showing the relationship between word pairs. Apply the same relationship to find the answer.

Cloze Passages

Fill in missing words based on context, grammar, and meaning. Read the whole passage first.

Word Classification

Group words by category or find the odd one out. Look for multiple types of relationships.
