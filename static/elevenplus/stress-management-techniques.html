Stress Management & Motivation

Manage exam anxiety and stay motivated during your 11+ preparation journey.

Start Practising

Breathing Techniques

Box Breathing: Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4. Repeat 5 times to calm your mind.

Positive Self-Talk

Replace negative thoughts with positive ones. "I can't do this" becomes "I can improve with practice."

Growth Mindset

View mistakes as learning opportunities. Each error helps you improve for next time.

Balanced Preparation

Study hard but maintain hobbies and social activities. Balance is essential for mental well-being.

Dealing with Setbacks

If you score poorly on a practice test, analyse what went wrong and adjust your study approach. One low score doesn't define your ability.

Mental well-being is as important as academic preparation. If you're struggling, speak to a parent, teacher, or school counsellor.
