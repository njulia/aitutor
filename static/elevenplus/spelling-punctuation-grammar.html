Spelling, Punctuation & Grammar

Avoid common SPaG errors that lose marks in 11+ exams.

Practice English Questions

Common Spelling Errors

Incorrect: occured
Correct: occurred (double c before -ed)
Incorrect: recieve
Correct: receive (i before e)

Punctuation Rules

Apostrophes: Use for contractions (don't, can't) and possessives (the cat's toy)

Commas: Separate items in lists and clauses

Speech marks: Use for direct speech

Colons & semicolons: Introduce lists or connect related clauses

Grammar Tips

Subject-verb agreement: The dog runs (not "dog run")

Verb tenses: Keep consistent tense throughout

Sentence structure: Avoid run-on sentences and fragments

Proofreading

Always leave time to check your work for spelling, punctuation, and grammar errors.

SPaG marks can make a real difference in 11+ results. Always leave a few minutes to proofread your work carefully.
