Mock Exam Strategy & Analysis

Maximise the value of mock exams to identify and improve weak areas in your 11+ preparation.

Take a Practice Test

Taking Mock Exams

Treat mocks like real exams: strict timing, quiet environment, no notes or help. This builds exam familiarity and confidence.

Analysing Your Results

After each mock:
  1. Identify questions you got wrong
  2. Categorise by topic (maths, English, reasoning)
  3. Identify patterns in your mistakes
  4. Plan targeted revision

Focused Revision

Don't revise everything equally. Spend more time on weak topics and less on areas where you already excel.

Timing Practice

Use mocks to practice pacing. If you consistently run out of time, practice working faster on easier questions.

Mock exams are one of the most effective preparation tools. Use them regularly and review results carefully for the best outcomes.
