GL Assessment vs CEM 11+: What Is the Difference?

Important: 11+ arrangements vary by school and region. Always check the current admissions and test information published by the school, consortium or local authority you are applying to.

Parents often search for GL Assessment vs CEM when choosing 11+ practice materials. The important point is that schools can use different assessment arrangements, and exam formats can change. Your first step should always be to check the current information for the schools you are targeting.

What can be tested?

11+ papers commonly assess areas such as English, Maths, verbal reasoning and non-verbal reasoning, but the exact combination, question style and timing can differ. Some schools or regions use consortium tests rather than a single national paper.

Why the distinction matters

Practice is most useful when it matches the type of questions and time pressure your child will actually face. Learning a broad skill such as vocabulary, arithmetic accuracy or pattern recognition is valuable regardless of the provider, but timed practice should reflect the target test.

How to prepare

  • Start with the school's published test information.
  • Build core English and Maths skills before chasing speed.
  • Practise verbal and non-verbal reasoning if required.
  • Use timed sets gradually so speed develops without sacrificing accuracy.
  • Review every mistake and record the underlying skill.
