Supporting Your Child's Preparation

How to help your child prepare for the 11+ without adding unnecessary stress.

Start Free Practice

Create a Study Environment

Do: Provide a quiet, organised study space

Do: Keep distractions minimal (phones, tablets)

Do: Ensure adequate lighting and comfort

Set Realistic Goals

Work with your child to set achievable targets. Celebrate progress, not just perfect scores. Focus on improvement rather than comparisons.

Maintain Balance

Important: Allow time for play, exercise, hobbies, and social activities. Mental health is as important as exam preparation.

Be Encouraging

Use positive language. Avoid phrases like "You must pass" or "This is your only chance." Instead, encourage effort and persistence.

Know When to Get Help

If your child is severely anxious or struggling significantly, consider professional support from a tutor or counsellor.

Your support and encouragement matter more than any practice paper. Keep the experience positive and focused on learning.
