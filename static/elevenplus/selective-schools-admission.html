Selective Schools Admission Process

Understanding how the 11+ admission process works in your local authority and independent schools.

Start Practising

Local Authority Grammar Schools

Managed by your local authority. Usually entrance exam in September/October of Year 6. Applications made through standard school admissions process.

Independent Grammar Schools

Each school manages its own entrance exams and applications. May have different dates and formats. Some charge entrance exam fees.

Key Dates

June Year 5: Check school websites for exam dates

July-August Year 5: Submit applications

September Year 6: Sit entrance exams

October-November Year 6: Receive results

December Year 6: Admissions finalised

Important Steps

Research schools early. Check exam boards and formats. Understand cut-off scores. Meet application deadlines. Keep records of applications and dates.
