Problem-Solving Techniques

Develop your problem-solving skills with strategies for tackling challenging questions.

Practice Problem Solving

The Problem-Solving Process

1. Read carefully - Understand what is being asked

2. Plan - Decide which operations to use

3. Work through it - Show all steps clearly

4. Check - Does the answer make sense?

Multi-Step Problems

Break problems into smaller steps. Solve one part at a time and use the answer to the first step in the next calculation.

Logical Reasoning

Use reasoning and trial-and-improvement for problems without obvious solutions. Work systematically and record what you've tried.

Practice Strategy

Work through challenging problems daily. Review mistakes carefully to understand where you went wrong.

Problem-solving is a skill that develops with practice. The more different types of problems you encounter, the more confident you'll become.
