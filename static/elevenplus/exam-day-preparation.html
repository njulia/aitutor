Exam Day Preparation

Your complete checklist for 11+ exam day success.

Take a Practice Test

The Night Before

  • Prepare your stationery (pens, pencils, eraser, ruler)
  • Check exam location and travel time
  • Get a good night's sleep
  • Avoid heavy revision - just review key concepts

Exam Morning

  • Eat a healthy breakfast
  • Arrive early to settle nerves
  • Use the toilet before entering the exam room
  • Do some light breathing exercises

During the Exam

  • Read instructions carefully
  • Start with questions you find easiest
  • Don't spend too long on difficult questions
  • Check your answers if time permits

Staying Calm

Remember: you've prepared well. Trust your training and do your best. One difficult question won't ruin your exam.

Being well-prepared on the day helps reduce anxiety and gives your child the best chance to perform at their level.
