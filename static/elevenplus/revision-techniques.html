Effective Revision Techniques

Evidence-based strategies to maximise retention and exam performance.

Start Practising

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks). This strengthens long-term memory.

Active Recall

Test yourself instead of just re-reading. Use flashcards, practice questions, or explain topics from memory.

Mind Mapping

Create visual representations of topics showing connections between ideas. Great for organising complex information.

Past Paper Practice

Work through actual past papers under exam conditions. This builds familiarity and identifies weak areas.

Interleaving

Mix different topics in study sessions rather than studying one topic at a time. This improves retention and transfer.

Create Your Revision Timetable

Plan regular revision sessions (not just cramming before the exam). Consistency beats intensity.

The most effective revision is regular, focused, and varied. Find what works best for your child and stick with it.
