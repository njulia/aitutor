Managing Test Anxiety

Reduce exam anxiety and build confidence for the 11+ exam with practical strategies for both students and parents.

Start Practicing

For Students

Practice exposure: Familiarity reduces anxiety. Take regular practice tests in exam-like conditions.

Breathing exercises: Use box breathing (4-4-4-4) to calm your nervous system.

Positive self-talk: Replace worry with encouragement.

For Parents

Normalise the exam: Treat it as one important test, not a life-defining moment.

Model calm: Children pick up on parental anxiety.

Focus on effort: Praise preparation and effort, not just results.

Physical Well-being

Regular exercise, sufficient sleep, and balanced nutrition all reduce anxiety. Don't skip these during exam prep.

Professional Support

If anxiety is severe, consider support from a school counsellor or therapist. There's no shame in asking for help.

These strategies are general guidance. If your child is experiencing severe anxiety, speak to their teacher or a healthcare professional.
