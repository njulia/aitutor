11+ Exam Dates & Registration Guide for 2027 Entry

Important: 11+ arrangements vary by school and region. Always check the current admissions and test information published by the school, consortium or local authority you are applying to.

For children aiming to start grammar school in September 2027, the 11+ test is usually taken during Year 5 or Year 6 depending on the area. There is no single UK-wide 11+ timetable: dates and registration windows vary by school, consortium and local authority.

The typical application sequence

  1. Research schools and their admissions rules.
  2. Check when 11+ registration opens and closes.
  3. Register for each test required by the schools you are targeting.
  4. Prepare for the relevant subjects and test format.
  5. Sit the entrance test and receive results.
  6. Submit the secondary-school application to your home local authority by its deadline.

Why dates matter

Missing an entrance-test registration deadline can prevent a child from sitting that test. Some regions also have unusually early tests or more than one stage. Keep a simple calendar with registration, test, results and school-application dates.

Where to check

Use the target school's official admissions page, the relevant grammar-school consortium and your home local authority. Third-party calendars can help you find dates, but the official source should be your final check because dates can change.
