How to choose from the shortlist

📍

Distance

Check how far the school is from home and how the journey would work each day.

📝

Admissions

Look for catchment rules, entrance tests, priority groups and application deadlines.

📊

Results

Use the official DfE performance service to compare recent results rather than relying on a single league-table number.

💛

School fit

Open days, subjects, pastoral support, activities and the daily journey can matter just as much as results.
