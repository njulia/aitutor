11+ Preparation Timeline

A 12-month structured plan to prepare for your 11+ exam.

Start Practising

Months 12-10: Foundation Building

Establish study habits. Review key concepts in English, Maths, and Reasoning. Take baseline practice tests to identify weak areas.

Months 9-7: Deep Dive

Focus on difficult topics. Increase practice test frequency. Practice essays and comprehension passages regularly.

Months 6-4: Consolidation

Build speed and accuracy. Practice under exam conditions. Review past papers from your target school's exam board.

Months 3-1: Final Push

Weekly practice tests. Intensive revision of weak areas. Finalise exam technique and time management strategies.

Adjust Your Plan

This is a general timeline. Adjust based on when your exam is and your specific needs.

This timeline is a guide. Start dates and exam dates vary by school and local authority. Always confirm exact dates with your target schools.
