Tutoring vs Self-Study

Understanding the pros and cons of different preparation approaches for the 11+.

Start Practising

Private Tutoring

Pros: Personalised attention, targeted feedback, flexible scheduling

Cons: Expensive, scheduling challenges, dependent on tutor quality

Group Classes

Pros: Less expensive, social support, structured curriculum

Cons: Less personalisation, fixed schedules

Self-Study with Resources

Pros: Cost-effective, flexible timing, builds independence

Cons: Requires self-discipline, harder to identify weak areas alone

How to Decide

Consider your child's learning style, finances, and areas of weakness. Many students benefit from a combination: self-study with targeted tutoring for difficult topics.

There is no single right approach. What works best depends on your child's needs, your budget, and the specific requirements of your target schools.
