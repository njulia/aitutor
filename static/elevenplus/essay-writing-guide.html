Essay Writing for 11+

Guidelines for writing persuasive and creative essays that impress examiners.

Practice Essay Writing

Essay Structure

Introduction: Introduce your topic clearly and state your main argument.

Body Paragraphs: Each paragraph should have one main idea with supporting evidence.

Conclusion: Summarise your arguments and restate your position.

Vocabulary Enhancement

Use varied vocabulary and avoid repetition. Show your understanding of advanced words naturally within sentences.

Common Pitfalls

  • Spelling and punctuation errors
  • Unclear thesis statement
  • Irrelevant examples
  • Poor paragraph structure
  • Weak conclusion

Practice Regularly

Write essays regularly and ask for feedback. Revise and improve your drafts.
