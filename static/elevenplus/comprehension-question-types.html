Types of Comprehension Questions

Understand the different question types that appear in the 11+ English comprehension section and the strategies to tackle each one effectively.

Practice Comprehension Questions

Literal Questions

What they ask: Information directly stated in the text

Strategy: Find the exact words or phrases in the passage

Inferential Questions

What they ask: What can you conclude from the text?

Strategy: Combine text clues with reasoning to draw conclusions

Vocabulary in Context

What they ask: What does this word mean?

Strategy: Use surrounding sentences to determine meaning, not a dictionary

Author's Purpose & Tone

What they ask: Why did the author write this? What's their attitude?

Strategy: Look for emotional words and consider the overall message

Cause & Effect

What they ask: Why did something happen?

Strategy: Find words like "because," "caused," "resulted in"
