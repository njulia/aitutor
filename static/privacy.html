Privacy notice

Homework Magic is designed for families. We aim to collect only the information needed to provide homework help, keep accounts secure and support parents or guardians.

A simple explanation for children

  • Ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or sharing information.
  • Use a nickname rather than your full name.
  • Do not share your school, home address, phone number, exact birthday or passwords.
  • Your parent or guardian can ask to see or delete information connected to your account.

Information we may use

The information depends on which features a family uses. It may include:

  • a parent or guardian email address and account security information;
  • a learner nickname, year group and selected learning preferences;
  • homework questions, answers, feedback, scores and progress records;
  • support messages sent through the Contact us page;
  • subscription status and Stripe customer or subscription identifiers; and
  • limited technical logs needed for security, reliability and fault-finding.

Payment-card details are entered on Stripe's hosted payment pages. Homework Magic does not need to store complete card details.

Why we use information

  • To create and secure parent or guardian accounts.
  • To provide homework, marking, explanations and progress features.
  • To remember the selected learner and appropriate year group.
  • To manage subscriptions and check access to paid features.
  • To answer support, billing and privacy questions.
  • To prevent misuse and keep the service working safely.

We do not use a child's homework or progress information to show targeted advertising.

Our legal reasons for using information

  • Contract: to create the parent or guardian account, supply requested learning features and manage a purchase or subscription.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain and improve the service, prevent misuse, respond to support requests and understand reliability, while taking extra care with children's information.
  • Legal obligations: for matters such as tax, accounting, fraud prevention, safeguarding or responding to a lawful request.
  • Consent: where we clearly ask for an optional use and it can be withdrawn, such as an optional feature that is not needed to provide the core service.

A parent or guardian manages the family account. Homework Magic is not intended for a child to create an account or purchase independently.

Who may process information

We may use carefully selected service providers to run the website, store data, provide AI features, send service emails, monitor faults and process payments. Providers should receive only the information needed for their task.

Depending on the feature and production configuration, these providers may include Google Cloud for hosting, databases and AI services; DeepSeek for selected AI review features; Brevo for service email; and Stripe for payment processing. We remain responsible for choosing and configuring processors appropriately.

Child profiles, homework, answers and progress should not be placed in Stripe payment metadata.

International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where required, we use an appropriate transfer mechanism and related safeguards, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual clauses.

Keeping and protecting information

We use access controls, encrypted connections, restricted administrator access and other technical safeguards intended to protect account and learning information. Parents and guardians should use a strong, unique password and should not share login details.

By default, the service stores scores and progress facts rather than the full text of a child's homework, answers or AI feedback. Raw learner or AI content should remain disabled unless a documented need and a suitable privacy assessment justify it.

Default retention periods

  • learning and progress records: up to 365 days;
  • optional learning memory: controlled by a parent or guardian, normally 365 days and no more than 730 days;
  • support messages: normally 180 days; and
  • password-reset links: short-lived and single-use, with expired records removed.

Some account, security, billing or accounting records may be kept for a different period where this is necessary for fraud prevention, disputes or a legal obligation.

Cookies and similar storage

Homework Magic uses essential browser cookies or local storage to keep a parent signed in, protect sessions, remember necessary settings and make account features work. We do not use behavioural advertising cookies. If non-essential analytics or advertising technology is introduced, families will be given the information and choices required by law before it is used.

AI-assisted homework help

AI may help generate or explain learning activities. It can make mistakes, so important decisions about a child should not be made only by AI. Parents, guardians and teachers should check important educational or safeguarding matters.

Clear identifiers are removed from model prompts where possible. Homework text is treated as untrusted data so that instructions hidden inside an uploaded document do not override the tutor's safety rules.

Homework Magic does not use AI output to make legal or similarly significant decisions about a child.

Parent and guardian choices

A parent or guardian may contact us to ask about:

  • accessing or correcting account and learner information;
  • deleting a learner profile or closing an account;
  • objecting to or restricting certain uses of information;
  • receiving a copy of relevant information; or
  • making a privacy complaint.

Some billing records may need to be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention or other legal obligations. An active subscription may need to be cancelled before an account can be fully closed.

If a privacy concern is not resolved, a parent or guardian may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
