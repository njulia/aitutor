What Year 4 children learn (KS2, ages 8–9)

Year 4 is when children are expected to know all their times tables up to 12×12, checked through the statutory Multiplication Tables Check taken by all Year 4 pupils in England.

  • All times tables up to 12×12
  • The Year 4 Multiplication Tables Check (MTC)
  • Decimals and simple fractions
  • Grammar: fronted adverbials, expanded noun phrases
  • Longer, more structured pieces of writing

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Multiplication Tables Check (MTC)?

It's a statutory online check, taken by all Year 4 pupils in England, testing quick recall of times tables facts up to 12×12 under timed conditions.

How can my child practise for the times tables check?

Short, regular timed practice — a few minutes a day — is generally more effective for building recall speed than occasional longer sessions.
