Year 1 Homework Help & Practice

Year 1 is an important step into primary school learning. Use Homework Magic for short, friendly practice in Maths, English, phonics and reading, alongside your child's school homework.

Start Year 1 practice

What can children practise in Year 1?

Homework Magic supports Maths, English, phonics, reading and age-appropriate Science. Use short practice sessions to reinforce school learning and help children become more confident.

  • Number and simple calculations
  • Phonics, spelling and early reading
  • Simple writing and sentence work
  • Science and everyday-world questions

Year 1 homework support

Use Homework Magic alongside your child's school homework. When a question is difficult, focus on understanding the method, then try another question. Reviewing mistakes can help children see which topics need more practice.

For families preparing for selective-school entrance, the separate 11+ section provides targeted practice, mistake review, topic mastery and mock exams.

Why use short practice sessions?

Short, focused activities can make it easier to keep practice manageable around school, reading, clubs and family time. Children can practise a skill, check their work and return to a tricky topic later.

Frequently asked questions about Year 1

What can a Year 1 child practise at home?

Year 1 practice can include Maths, phonics, reading, spelling and simple writing, alongside the homework set by school.

How long should Year 1 practice take?

Short, regular practice is often easier for young children than long sessions. Keep activities manageable and include time for reading.

Can Homework Magic help with Year 1 homework?

Yes. Homework Magic provides friendly practice and explanations for primary school learning, including Year 1 topics.
