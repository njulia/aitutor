Year 3 Homework Help & Practice

Year 3 is the start of KS2. Children meet new Maths and English challenges and begin to build stronger independent study habits. Homework Magic offers short practice and friendly support.

Start Year 3 practice

What can children practise in Year 3?

Homework Magic supports Maths, English reading, writing, spelling, grammar and Science. Use short practice sessions to reinforce school learning and help children become more confident.

  • Multiplication and division foundations
  • Fractions and problem solving
  • Reading comprehension and vocabulary
  • Grammar, spelling and writing

Year 3 homework support

Use Homework Magic alongside your child's school homework. When a question is difficult, focus on understanding the method, then try another question. Reviewing mistakes can help children see which topics need more practice.

For families preparing for selective-school entrance, the separate 11+ section provides targeted practice, mistake review, topic mastery and mock exams.

Why use short practice sessions?

Short, focused activities can make it easier to keep practice manageable around school, reading, clubs and family time. Children can practise a skill, check their work and return to a tricky topic later.

Frequently asked questions about Year 3

What should Year 3 children practise?

Year 3 practice can include Maths, multiplication and division, fractions, reading comprehension, spelling, grammar and writing.

Does Homework Magic have Year 3 Maths practice?

Yes. Homework Magic has Year 3 Maths practice and can provide additional practice around school homework.

Can Year 3 children start 11+ preparation?

Some families begin exploring 11+ skills during KS2. Homework Magic also has a separate 11+ area for families who choose to prepare.
