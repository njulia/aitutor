Your safety matters

Homework can wait. You should never have to deal with a scary or unsafe situation on your own.

Tell a trusted adult now

Tell a parent, carer, teacher or another grown-up you trust. Keep telling a safe adult until someone helps.

If you or somebody else is in immediate danger, call 999.

Childline

You can call Childline free on 0800 1111. You can talk about anything that is worrying you.

Visit Childline

Privacy tip

Do not type your full name, school, home address, phone number, exact birthday or password into homework boxes. Ask a parent or guardian for help.
