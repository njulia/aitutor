Questions from parents

11 Plus mock exam FAQs

Which subjects are in the online 11+ mocks?

Common mocks cover Maths, English, Verbal Reasoning and Non-Verbal Reasoning. School-target simulations follow the scope described on each mock card.

Which school and area-target 11 Plus mocks are available?

The catalogue includes independent practice for QE Barnet, Tiffin School, Tiffin Girls, Wilson's, St Olave's, Henrietta Barnett, Altrincham Girls, Reading, CCHS, Kent, Buckinghamshire, Sutton, West Midlands, CSSE Essex, Lancaster, Bexley, Wirral, Gloucestershire, Slough Consortium and Medway formats.

Are these official school exam papers?

No. Every question is original Homework Magic practice. Named-school simulations are independent and are not endorsed by a school.

How much do the full mock exams cost?

The Common 11+ Diagnostic costs nothing. Every other mock is included only with the 11+ Premium subscription at £9.99 per month.

Public-source promise

Original questions, checked against public information

Homework Magic does not copy paid papers, familiarisation questions or school exam questions. Public government, council and school pages are used only to check curriculum scope and published formats. School and area-target mocks are independent practice simulations, not official papers and not endorsed by the schools, councils or test providers.
