A parent-controlled plan

Keep the wider child in view.

  • Protect sleep, reading for pleasure, play and family time
  • Use short sessions with a clear stopping point
  • Review mistakes without shame or fear-based language
  • Check target-school admissions information separately
  • Pause or reduce practice when stress is building
